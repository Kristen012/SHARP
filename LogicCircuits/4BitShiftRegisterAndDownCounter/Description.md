## 4BitShiftRegisterAndDownCounter

* **Description**

    This design operates a four-bit register to either shift in serial data or decrement the stored value as a down counter based on active enable signals.

* **Input Type**

    input_x: bool

* **Output Type**

    o_result: sc_uint<4>
