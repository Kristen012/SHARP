## Lemmings4

* **Description**

    This design manages walking, falling, digging, and splatting states using a counter to track fall duration. It evaluates ground, bump, and dig signals to determine and output active movement statuses.

* **Input Type**

    input_x: bool

* **Output Type**

    o_result: bool
